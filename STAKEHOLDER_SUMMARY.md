# CareFlow Analytics & Insights: Executive Stakeholder Summary

## Project Overview
The CareFlow Clinical Process Mining project analyzes patient pathways, departmental transit times, and clinical bottlenecks to optimize operational efficiency and improve healthcare delivery.

## Key Findings & Performance Metrics
1. **Bottleneck Isolation:** Identified high-latency transition points in Emergency to ICU handoffs, reducing patient waiting time uncertainty.
2. **Resource & Workload Distribution:** Evaluated physician and nursing staff allocation to balance departmental workloads during peak influx hours.
3. **Data Integrity & Imputation:** Applied leakage-free imputation and scaling pipelines to maintain audit-ready clinical event logs.

## Strategic Recommendations
- **Automated Routing:** Implement dynamic routing for high-acuity patients to minimize triage delays.
- **Resource Reallocation:** Shift shift-schedules to match peak emergency department intake times.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** Deploy real-time process mining dashboards for clinical team managers.
